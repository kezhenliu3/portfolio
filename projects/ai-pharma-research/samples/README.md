# Sample Reports — Desensitization Statement

Two full reports generated by the AI Pharma Research Platform, published here for demonstration.

## Desensitization statement

These reports were reviewed page-by-page before publication:

- **Text layer**: scanned for internal infrastructure patterns (internal IPs, internal hosts, local paths, internal tool names, API keys) — zero hits.
- **Metadata**: no author/title fields, no embedded forms or scripts (generated by WeasyPrint only).
- **Links**: every URL is a public, authoritative source (clinicaltrials.gov, WHO GLOBOCAN, SEER, NCBI/PMC, peer-reviewed journals, public news).
- **Content**: confirmed to contain only public literature and market information. The "license-in / license-out" transactions described are public business-development news, not internal decisions.
- **Images**: charts and figures are generated from public data; no internal screenshots or watermarks.

**What is deliberately not published:** source markdown (contains internal image-server URLs), target-type reports (contain internal development strategy chapters), scoring reports, and all internal tooling.

## What to look for

1. **Stable structure across indications** — compare the two reports: the same template drives both, with identical chapter skeletons.
2. **Citation discipline** — numbered citations throughout, matched to public sources.
3. **Data depth** — epidemiology, treatment landscape, market size, and competitor tables per template requirements.
4. **Evidence grading** — authoritative sources (GLOBOCAN, SEER, NCCN, peer-reviewed literature) carry the claims.

*These files were produced by the platform itself; nothing was hand-written or fabricated for demonstration.*
